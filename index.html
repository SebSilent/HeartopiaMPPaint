Controls

  • Drag to paint. Every finger paints on its own, so two-finger drawing works.
  • B brush · E eraser · I eyedropper · F bucket · L line · R rectangle · O ellipse · A arrow · V select
  • [ ] brush size · M mirror · G grid · 0 fit · + − zoom
  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo, one step per stroke, shape or fill.
  • Zoom: mouse wheel, or pinch with two fingers. Two fingers never paint, so you can rest your hand on the screen.
  • Pan: two-finger drag, hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle or right button.
  • With a stylus down, finger touches are ignored, so palm rejection comes free.
  • Shift constrains a line to 45 degrees and a rectangle to a square.

A cursor shows the tool its owner is using. People working another face appear on that face's tab.
