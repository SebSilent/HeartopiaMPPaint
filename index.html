Controls

  • Drag to paint. Every finger paints on its own, so two-finger drawing works.
  • B brush · E eraser · I eyedropper · H hand
  • [ ] brush size · G grid · 0 fit · + − zoom
  • Zoom: wheel, or pinch with two fingers while the hand tool is active.
  • Pan: hold Space and drag, or drag with the middle or right button.
  • Ctrl+S saves the board as a PNG at its native pixel size.

Cursors are drawn as the tool the other player is actually using: arrow for a mouse, nib for a stylus, a disc for a finger.
